How to Play

👆

Swap to match

Swipe adjacent tiles. Only swaps that make a Match-3+ (or activate a special) are allowed.

🌩️

Build specials

Match-4 = Lightning Cloud. L/T of 5 = Sun King. Straight 5 = Rainbow Star.

⚡

Fill the Blitz Meter

Every clear adds charge. Hit 100% to freeze the timer and score 3× for 5 seconds.

🦸

Hero power

Clear your hero emoji to charge their unique spell. Tap the hero icon when ready.

🪙

Soft currency only

Coins and gems are earned in-game. No real-money purchases — ever.

🎁

Daily rewards

Login gifts, hourly crates, spins, and events keep the collection growing.

🏆

Missions

Complete mission rows to earn XP toward chest rewards and level tabs.

📦

Store boxes

Silver, Gold, Diamond, and Series boxes contain new emojis and bonuses.
