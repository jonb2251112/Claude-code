How to Play

👆

Swap to match

Swipe adjacent tiles. Only swaps that make a Match-3+ (or activate a special) are allowed.

🌩️

Build specials

Match-4 = Lightning Cloud. L/T of 5 = Sun King. Straight 5 = Rainbow Star. Combine specials for huge wipes.

⚡

Fill the Blitz Meter

Every clear adds charge. Hit 100% to freeze the timer, shrink the pool, and score 3× for 5 seconds.

🦸

Hero power

Clear your hero emoji to charge their unique spell. Tap the hero icon when ready.

🪙

Earn & unlock

Coins unlock new characters. Chase high scores and cascade combos.
