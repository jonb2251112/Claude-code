# MAP BREAKDOWN — BANK
*Downtown Los Angeles · Launch map (2015) · Ranked pool staple*

Three playable levels plus a roof. Bank is the definitive "vertical +
basement" map: an open, sightline-heavy first floor, a tight and
claustrophobic basement, and a top floor that punishes anyone who
forgets to look up.

---
## LAYOUT

### Exterior & Spawns
- **Boulevard** — front of the building. Main doors, ATM windows.
  Longest sightlines, most spawn-peek risk.
- **Parking Lot** — west side. Feeds the Garage ramp and 1F windows.
- **Back Alley / Loading Dock** — rear. Closest approach to the
  Staff Room / Open Area half of the building.
- **Roof** — reached by rappel. Skylight over the Lobby, hatches down
  into the 2F offices.

## THE FOUR BOMB SITES

### 1. Basement — **CCTV Room / Lockers** ★ Best defender site
- **Approaches:** Main Stairs, Blue Stairs, Garage side, and **hatches
  from 1F** dropped into the objective ring.
- **Defender priority:** reinforce the two hatches above the objective,
  the Lockers wall toward Vault Lobby, and the CCTV–Server wall.
- **Electrify** the main hard-breach wall (Bandit battery / Kaid claw) —
  without a Thatcher, attackers often cannot open the basement at all.
- **Rotate:** cut Lockers ↔ CCTV so the two anchors can trade rooms
  without touching the hallway.
- **Power positions:** Server Room (holds CCTV entry), Vault Lobby
  (holds Lockers entry), top of Blue Stairs (roam pinch).
- **Default plant spots:** deep Lockers behind the metal lockers, or the
  back-left corner of CCTV — both out of line from the stair entries.
- **Attack plan:** clear roamers on 1F first, take **both stairwells**
  simultaneously so defenders cannot rotate, open a hatch for a third
  angle, then breach the reinforced wall with the drone already inside.

### 2. 1F — **Tellers' Office / Archives**
- **Approaches:** Lobby, Open Area, and **vertically from 2F**.
- **Defender priority:** reinforce the hatches above Tellers' and
  Archives — this site loses to vertical play more than to any entry.
- **Power positions:** behind the teller counters, Archives shelving.
- **Plant spots:** behind the Tellers' counter; Archives back corner.
- **Attack plan:** Sledge/Buck open the 2F floor, hold the hole, then
  push Lobby once the anchors are forced off their angles.

### 3. 1F — **Open Area / Staff Room** ★ Hardest to defend
- **Approaches:** Back Alley windows, Lobby, Garage, and 2F ceilings.
- **Defender priority:** this site is too open to hold statically —
  reinforce the ceiling hatches, cut deep rotates, and play **outside**
  the site (Lobby, Tellers') to catch attackers crossing.
- **Plant spots:** Staff Room corner behind the kitchen units.
- **Attack plan:** the fastest site on the map. Flood two entries, plant
  early, and win the post-plant with numbers.

### 4. 2F — **CEO Office / Executive Lounge**
- **Approaches:** Terrace rappel, roof hatches, Stock Trading, Meeting
  Room, Top Stairs.
- **Defender priority:** reinforce the CEO wall toward Stock Trading,
  the Lounge wall toward Front Desk, and every hatch from the roof.
  **Mira on the CEO–Stock wall** is the classic setup.
- **Power positions:** Exec Hallway (rotation control), Janitor (flank),
  Meeting Room (backline crossfire).
- **Plant spots:** CEO behind the desk; Lounge behind the couches.
- **Attack plan:** control the Terrace and the roof first. Without roof
  and Terrace, you are pushing three doorways into crossfire and you
  will lose. Hard breach CEO from Stock Trading with Thatcher escort.

## BANK SIGNATURE INTERACTIONS
- **Skylight → Lobby:** attackers can shoot down into the Lobby from the
  roof, controlling the whole central atrium.
- **Elevators:** a vertical noise trap — footsteps carry between floors
  around the shaft, giving free vertical callouts to both sides.
- **Garage ramp:** the only "quiet" approach toward the basement; a
  Kapkan or Frost trap there wins rounds outright.
- **Bulletproof teller glass:** blocks bullets but not explosives —
  a Fuze or Ash round resets that entire angle.
- **Basement is a sound box:** every footstep echoes. Crouch-walking in
  the basement is not optional, it is the whole skill of the site.
