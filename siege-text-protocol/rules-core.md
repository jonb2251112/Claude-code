# CORE SYSTEMS

## 1. Match Structure (Ranked)
- **First to 4 round wins** takes the match.
- **Sides swap after Round 3.**
- **3–3 → Overtime:** up to 3 extra rounds, sides swap entering OT.
  First team to **5 total round wins** takes the match (max score 5–4).
- **Ban Phase:** each team bans 1 attacker and 1 defender (2 + 2 total).
  Banned operators are locked out for the entire match.
- **Op Select:** 5 unique operators per side, no duplicates.

## 2. Round Timings (exact)
| Event | Time |
|---|---|
| Preparation phase | 45s |
| Action phase | 3:00 |
| Defuser plant | 7s (+0.5s start animation) |
| Defuser countdown | 45s |
| Defender disable | 7s (+0.5s) — must finish inside the 45s |
| Downed (DBNO) bleedout | ~60s |

- The **plant animation may finish after the action-phase timer expires**.
- The **disable must complete within the 45s** or the defuser detonates.
- Two bombs sit in **adjoining rooms**; attackers only need one.
- Each map has **4 bomb-site pairs**; one is chosen at random per round.

## 3. Health & Speed
Armor was replaced by flat HP. Speed still governs movement.

| Tier | HP | Movement |
|---|---|---|
| 3 Speed / 1 Armor | 100 | Fastest |
| 2 Speed / 2 Armor | 110 | Standard |
| 1 Speed / 3 Armor | 125 | Slowest |

- **DBNO (Downed But Not Out):** 50 HP, crawl only, revivable.
- **Rook plates:** +20 bonus HP and greatly increased DBNO chance.
- **Doc stim:** heals 40 (can overheal above base HP) or self/ally revive.
- Headshots are **instant kill** at any range with any bullet weapon.

## 4. Destruction Tiers
1. **Barricades** — wood planks on doors/windows. Melee, bullets, any
   explosive, Sledge hammer. Breaking one is loud and directional.
2. **Soft surfaces** (drywall, plaster, wood floors) — bullets punch
   pinholes and penetrate; shotguns, hammers, impacts and breach charges
   open man-sized holes. Applies to walls, **floors and ceilings**.
3. **Hard surfaces** (concrete, load-bearing, metal shutters) — indestructible.
4. **Reinforced walls** — soft walls plated by defenders. Only **hard
   breachers** open them: Thermite, Hibana, Maverick, Ace. 2 reinforcements
   per defender (10 total per round), placed in prep phase.
5. **Hatches** — floor openings. Soft by default (explosives, Sledge,
   shotgun, Buck). Reinforced hatches require hard breach.
6. **Bulletproof deployables** — Deployable Shield, Castle Armor Panels,
   Mira Black Mirror, Goyo shield. Removed by explosives (or by
   destroying the wall around them).

**Rotation holes** are as important as breaches: defenders pre-cut
soft-wall rotates so they can move between rooms without using hallways.

## 5. Sound & Information Warfare
Sound is the primary intel channel. Every audible event is broadcast
with direction *and vertical layer* (above / same floor / below).

**Loud (heard through walls, 2 rooms+):**
- Sprinting footsteps · barricade break · reinforcement placement
- Wall/hatch breach detonation · shotgun blasts · Sledge hammer swings
- Rappelling onto a wall · window vaults · Thermite/Hibana charge burn

**Medium (heard ~1 room):**
- Walking footsteps · reloads · gadget deployment (jammer, ADS, cam)
- Drone motor whir · barbed wire crunch · door opening

**Quiet (same room / very close):**
- Crouch-walking · defuser pickup · leaning · ADS-activation click

**Silent:** standing still, aiming, using a camera/drone remotely.

**Counter-audio play:** crouch-walk to hold an angle, cover a rotate
with simultaneous team gunfire, drop a shield to mask a reload, or spam
a barricade to bait a callout.

## 6. Intel Systems
- **Drones:** each attacker carries **2** — one deployed in prep, one
  reserved for the action phase. Drones can jump, be shot, be jammed
  (Mute), be spotted by defenders on cameras, and are destroyed by melee.
- **Prep-phase drones survive into the action phase** if left alive.
- **Security cameras:** fixed cams around the map, usable by all
  defenders; attackers destroy them by shooting the lens (a destroyed
  cam is a callout in itself).
- **Pings:** a ping marks a position/enemy for the whole team.
- **Bomb spotting:** droning both bomb rooms reveals the site to the team.

## 7. Gunplay Fundamentals
- **Peeker's advantage** — the moving player sees the static player
  fractionally first. Wide-swinging beats slow-peeking in a duel; holding
  an angle is only strong when the angle is *tight*.
- **Recoil** is vertical-then-horizontal; controlled by burst discipline.
- **Attachments:** sights (Red Dot / Holo / Reflex / 1.5x / 2.5x /
  Scope 2.5x), barrels (Suppressor, Flash Hider, Compensator, Muzzle
  Brake, Extended Barrel), grips (Vertical, Angled, Horizontal),
  and Laser (faster ADS/hipfire accuracy, but a visible red beam).
- **Suppressor** removes the directional gunshot callout at range but
  reduces damage — a roamer's tool.
- **Leaning (Q/E)** exposes less of your body; **prone** is a last resort.
- **Rappel** — attackers can rappel exterior walls, run horizontally,
  jump around corners, and swing in through windows head-first
  (a "rappel entry"), which is loud but fast.

## 8. Utility Economy
The round is won by the team that spends utility better.

- **Hard breach vs. denial** — Thermite/Hibana/Ace/Maverick must open a
  reinforced wall; Bandit/Kaid/Mute must stop them. Thatcher/Twitch/
  Kali/IQ exist to break that denial.
- **Utility must be spent on the objective**, not wasted clearing rooms.
- **Post-plant denial** — Smoke gas, Nitro Cell, Echo drones and Kapkan
  traps convert a lost site into a won round.
