# ATTACKERS

## ASH — Eliza Cohen · Israel / FBI SWAT · 3 Speed / 100 HP
- **Primary:** R4-C · G36C
- **Secondary:** 5.7 USG · M45 MEUSOC
- **Gadget slot:** Breach Charge / Claymore
- **Unique — M120 CREM Breaching Round (x2)**

Underbarrel launcher firing two impact-detonating breaching rounds.
Opens a large hole in any soft wall, barricade, hatch or Castle panel
**from range**, and destroys most defender gadgets in the blast.

- Cannot damage reinforced walls or hard surfaces.
- Deals ~25 blast damage to operators — enough to soften, not to kill.
- **Countered by:** Jäger's ADS (a Magpie swats it out of the air),
  Wamai discs, and bulletproof cover.
- **Why she's S-tier:** fastest entry frag in the game, opens the site
  from a safe distance, and clears traps on the way in.

## THERMITE — Jordan Trace · USA / FBI SWAT · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** 556xi · M1014
- **Secondary:** 5.7 USG · M45 MEUSOC
- **Gadget slot:** Stun Grenade / Smoke Grenade
- **Unique — Exothermic-321 Breaching Charge (x2)**

Places on a **reinforced wall or reinforced hatch**, burns for a few
seconds, then blows a full man-sized opening — the largest and cleanest
hard breach in the game.

- Will **not stick** to an electrified surface (Bandit/Kaid), and is
  destroyed if the wall is electrified while burning ("Bandit trick").
- Blocked by a Mute jammer in range.
- Only 2 charges — every wasted charge is a lost round.
- **Needs an escort:** Thatcher, Twitch, Kali or IQ to clear denial.

## THATCHER — Mike Baker · UK / SAS · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** AR33 · L85A2 · M590A1
- **Secondary:** P226 MK 25
- **Gadget slot:** Breach Charge / Claymore
- **Unique — EG MKO EMP Grenade (x2)**

Thrown grenade that disables and destroys defender **electronics** in a
radius — **through walls, floors and ceilings**.

- Kills: Bandit batteries, Kaid Electroclaws, Mute jammers, Jäger ADS,
  Valkyrie cams, bulletproof cams, Kapkan/Ela/Aruni devices, Echo drones.
- Does **not** affect non-electronic gadgets (Mira's Black Mirror glass,
  barbed wire, Goyo shields).
- Only 2 grenades — a Thatcher who throws both at the wrong wall has
  ended his team's hard breach.

## SLEDGE — Seamus Cowden · UK / SAS · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** M590A1 · L85A2
- **Secondary:** P226 MK 25 · SMG-11
- **Gadget slot:** Frag Grenade / Stun Grenade
- **Unique — Tactical Breaching Hammer "The Caber" (25 charges)**

Melee tool that opens soft walls, floors, ceilings, barricades and
**unreinforced hatches** in a few swings.

- Swings are **loud and directional** — every hit is a free callout for
  defenders.
- Cannot touch reinforced surfaces or hard walls.
- **The vertical king:** open a ceiling above site, hold the hole, and
  force defenders off their anchor spots.

## TWITCH — Emmanuelle Pichon · France / GIGN · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** F2 · 417 · SG-CQB
- **Secondary:** P9 · LFP586
- **Gadget slot:** Claymore / Smoke Grenade
- **Unique — RSD Model 1 Shock Drone (x2)**

A second, tougher drone armed with taser darts.

- **3 darts per drone**, recharging ~1 dart every 30s.
- Darts destroy defender gadgets and deal 1 damage + heavy flinch to
  operators (interrupts plants, reinforcements and reloads).
- **Deployable in the preparation phase** and survives into the action
  phase — the premier tool for stripping Bandit batteries and jammers
  before the hard breacher even walks up.
- **Countered by:** Mute jammers, Vigil, melee, and defenders simply
  shooting it.

## HIBANA — Yumiko Imagawa · Japan / SAT · 3 Speed / 100 HP
- **Primary:** TYPE-89 · SUPERNOVA
- **Secondary:** P229 · Bearing 9
- **Gadget slot:** Stun Grenade / Breach Charge
- **Unique — X-KAIROS Launcher (18 pellets)**

Fires sticky thermal pellets in **2×1, 2×2 or 3×2** grids that adhere to
reinforced walls and hatches, then detonate on a short fuse.

- A **3×2 volley (6 pellets)** cuts a man-sized hole.
- Can breach **from a distance** and **from below** (hatch breaching) —
  Thermite cannot.
- Pellets fail on uneven/non-flush surfaces and around obstructions.
- **Countered by:** Bandit/Kaid electricity (pellets are destroyed before
  detonation), Mute jammers, and Wamai/ADS on the volley.

## ZOFIA — Zofia Bosak · Poland / GROM · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** LMG-E · M762
- **Secondary:** RG15
- **Gadget slot:** Breach Charge / Claymore
- **Unique — KS79 Lifeline (4 rounds: 2 Impact + 2 Concussion)**
- **Passive — Withstand:** self-revives once from DBNO at low HP.

Impact rounds open soft walls/hatches and destroy gadgets at range.
Concussion rounds disorient (heavy blur, slowed movement) through a
small radius — a plant-denial and site-entry tool in one.

- Both round types are intercepted by ADS/Wamai.
- Withstand makes her the safest solo entry — but a defender who watches
  the body and re-downs her wastes her whole value.

## MONTAGNE — Gilles Touré · France / GIGN · 1 Speed / 125 HP
- **Primary:** Le Roc Extendable Shield
- **Secondary:** P9 · LFP586
- **Gadget slot:** Smoke Grenade / Impact EMP Grenade
- **Unique — Le Roc:** full-body shield that extends to cover the
  operator completely.

- Extended: **immune to all frontal bullet damage**, cannot shoot, moves
  slowly, and can push defenders out of position or body-block a plant.
- Retracted: normal shield with a pistol.
- **Countered by:** melee/nitro from behind, Kapkan/Ela/Lesion traps,
  explosive damage, Nomad airjabs, Oryx dashes, and getting flanked.
- **His real job:** absorb the first duel and *give information* while
  the team enters behind him.

# DEFENDERS

## JÄGER — Marius Streicher · Germany / GSG 9 · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** 416-C Carbine · M870
- **Secondary:** P12
- **Gadget slot:** Barbed Wire / Bulletproof Camera
- **Unique — ADS Mk III "Magpie" (x3)**

Placed on floors or walls; automatically shoots down incoming thrown
or launched projectiles.

- **Each Magpie intercepts 2 projectiles, then shuts down permanently.**
- Stops frags, stuns, smokes, impacts, Ash rounds, Zofia rounds, Ying
  candelas, Fuze sub-grenades, Nomad airjabs, GONNE-6 shots.
- **Countered by:** Thatcher EMP, Twitch darts, IQ (spots it through
  walls), Kali LV lance, or simply **overloading it** — throw three
  grenades, the third gets through.

## BANDIT — Dominic Brunsmeier · Germany / GSG 9 · 3 Speed / 100 HP
- **Primary:** MP7 · M870
- **Secondary:** P12 · Bailiff 410
- **Gadget slot:** Barbed Wire / Nitro Cell
- **Unique — CED-1 Shock Wire (x4 batteries)**

Electrifies **reinforced walls, barbed wire and deployable shields**.

- Destroys any breach charge or Hibana pellet placed on the electrified
  surface, and shocks attackers who touch the wire/shield.
- **"Bandit tricking":** removing and re-placing the battery at the exact
  moment a hard breach is about to fire, so the charge dies mid-burn.
- **Countered by:** Thatcher EMP, Twitch darts, IQ, Kali, and Maverick
  (his blowtorch cuts the wall without ever placing a device on it).
- Fastest anchor in the game — doubles as an aggressive roamer.

## MUTE — Mark Chandar · UK / SAS · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** MP5K · M590A1
- **Secondary:** P226 MK 25 · SMG-11
- **Gadget slot:** Nitro Cell / Bulletproof Camera
- **Unique — GC90 Signal Disruptor (x4 jammers)**

A signal bubble that:
- Jams attacker **drones and remote gadgets** inside it
- **Prevents breach charges and Hibana pellets from being placed/fired**
- Blocks Thermite charges, Fuze remote detonation, and Dokkaebi calls
- **Denies the prep-phase drone**, hiding the bomb site entirely

Jammers can be shot, EMP'd, or destroyed by Twitch. Their hum is
audible — a jammer placed in the open is a free kill for the attackers.

## SMOKE — James Porter · UK / SAS · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** FMG-9 · M590A1
- **Secondary:** P226 MK 25 · SMG-11
- **Gadget slot:** Barbed Wire / Deployable Shield
- **Unique — Compound Z8 Remote Gas Grenade (x3)**

Thrown canisters detonated remotely at any time, creating a toxic cloud
that deals heavy damage over time.

- Gas **ignores cover and shields** — it flows into the space.
- Damages Smoke himself and his own teammates.
- The single best **post-plant denial** tool: gas the defuser and the
  attackers cannot disable it.
- **Countered by:** killing Smoke before he detonates, or pre-firing the
  canister; there is no gadget that removes the cloud.

## MIRA — Elena "Álvarez" María Álvarez · Spain / GEO · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** Vector .45 ACP · ITA12L
- **Secondary:** USP40 · ITA12S
- **Gadget slot:** Nitro Cell / Proximity Alarm
- **Unique — Black Mirror (x2)**

A one-way bulletproof window installed into a soft **or reinforced** wall.

- Defenders see through it; attackers see only black glass.
- A **gas canister** on the defender's side can be punched to eject the
  window, turning it into an open firing port (permanent — it cannot be
  re-sealed).
- **Countered by:** shooting the canister from the attacker side (it is
  visible), or blowing up the wall around the mirror.
- Transforms a site: a Mira on the correct wall makes an entire approach
  unwalkable.

## VALKYRIE — Meghan J. Castellano · USA / Navy SEALs · 2 Speed / 110 HP
- **Primary:** MPX · SPAS-12
- **Secondary:** D-50
- **Gadget slot:** Nitro Cell / Impact Grenade
- **Unique — Black Eye (x3)**

Throwable sticky cameras usable by the whole defending team.

- Can be thrown **outside** through windows onto exterior walls for
  spawn-peek-level intel, or stuck in ceiling corners on site.
- Cameras placed too low or in obvious spots die instantly.
- **Countered by:** Thatcher EMP, Twitch darts, IQ, and shooting them.
- Best value: one cam on site, one on the flank, one outside.

## ROOK — Julien Nizan · France / GIGN · 1 Speed / 125 HP
- **Primary:** MP5 · SG-CQB
- **Secondary:** P9 · LFP586
- **Gadget slot:** Impact Grenade / Proximity Alarm
- **Unique — R1N "Rhino" Armor Plates (bag of 5)**

Drops a plate bag; each defender (including Rook) can take one plate.

- **+20 HP** and a dramatically increased chance of going **DBNO**
  instead of dying outright.
- Auto-equips on pickup and lasts the whole round.
- **The correct play is to drop the bag immediately** in prep phase in a
  central spot so roamers can grab a plate before leaving site.
- No counter — this is pure, unremovable team value.

## KAID — Jalal El Fassi · Morocco / GIGR · 1 Speed / 125 HP
- **Primary:** AUG A3 · TCSG12
- **Secondary:** LFP586 · .44 Mag Semi-Auto
- **Gadget slot:** Barbed Wire / Observation Blocker
- **Unique — Rtila Electroclaw (x2)**

Thrown claw that electrifies **all connected metal surfaces in range** —
reinforced walls, hatches, barbed wire and deployable shields.

- Can be **thrown onto ceilings and hatches**, letting him electrify a
  hatch from the floor below — something Bandit cannot do.
- Covers far more surface than a Bandit battery, but only 2 devices and
  it can be thrown badly.
- **Countered by:** Thatcher EMP, Twitch, IQ, Kali, Maverick.
- Kaid + Bandit together is the classic "double denial" wall setup.
